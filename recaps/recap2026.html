2026 Draft Recap
You all know what time it is- do I really have to introduce it? It's the 2026 Draft Recap! For this year's theme we are going back to an old classic. Of course I am talking about the movie we all love and cherish, Dumb & Dumber. Each team gets assigned a minor character on the way that I, the commish, feel their draft best represents. So without further ado, let's get into it!

As always, this is all in good fun- don't take any of the criticisms or praise to heart, just having some fun.
  • Schteeve - "Lovely Accent Girl" (rating: 6.5)
    Just like the movie starts out, Schteeve was on Hope St coming into the draft. The dreadful "Leon Years" were over and he had a chance to finally make something of his roster that could be good. However, like the limo driver, he got a little confused as he took some players that desperately need to return to their 2024 form? However if they can, then he'll be able to go "over da bridge" and into the promise land of the playoffs- but if they don't, well, playoffs? Let's not.
  • Tyler - Lil Ol' Lady On a Motorized Cart (8.5)
    Yo, Ty came out swinging this year and I didn't even see it coming! He made off with some sweet stuff, like a ping pong paddle, some booze, and oh yeah, Kyren Williams, Pickens, a couple Warrens, Caleb and Jordan Addison way down at pick #100. Tyler definitely is serving his purpose of trying to make his first playoffs and maybe even win his first championship in the process. But now that I've pumped up his team I guess I have to say to him, "Don't you go dying on me!"
  • Kevin - Billy in 4C (5.0)
    Some picks that he made were nice and others made me wonder if he was blind or something. Kevin had nice keepers in JSN and Lamb (the trade was agreed to before he kept Collins) so all he had to do was compliment them to put together a championship squad. So of course he drafts... a high risk rookie RB in Love? Then two Bears back-to-back, in which, even as a Bears fan, they won't be able to fully support an entire fantasy roster (Odunze is slated to still get more looks than Luther.) In any case his eyes probably lit up when he landed Drake Maye at #66, but the guy kind of lost his head on opening night didn't he?
  • Travis - Sea Bass (4.5)
    The Big Bad Champion coming in hot, trying to intimidate us all with possibly the top TWO WRs in the game. But then after that first appearance he was kind of, not that scary in the end? I mean a bathroom stall door knocked out the character Sea Bass cold in the movie right? Well we might knock out Travis's team if his two keepers fail to put up monster scores. Skattebo and Pollard at RB? Pitts and TE? Drafting a guy named Strange just because of his name? Time to throw some salt shakers boys, let's get a new champion!
  • Greg - Icelandic Snow Owl (2.5)
    What should be a beautiful draft that we normally see from one of our all-time winningest managers turned out to be basically DOA in the end. I guess Greg is playing for another life as he lay dead in his cage on this one with picks like McConkey, Higgins, and McLaurin to start his draft. Just underperformer city going on here and it's not like these guys are that young with potential. We already know what most of them have in the bag and it's not ELITE performances. McBride and CMC have some work cut out for them, but unlike the Icelandic Snow Owls, I wouldn't go out of my way to preserve this lineup.
  • Tony - Mentalino "aka" Mental (8.5)
    I would love to trash Tony's draft, would love to- however he did exactly what needs to be done to win the league, go heavy on RBs. I even tried to divert his attention by calling him out in the chat to make him stop- but he just kept on going like a possessed hitman. The guy has gas... but by gas I mean fuel in the tank to win it all in not only his RBs but a healthy Burrow at QB and Kelce in his last hurrah at TE. There are some Q's at WR so maybe he was at the wrong place at the wrong time for those but we're going to need to put some red hots and rat poison in his team to get him to kick the bucket somehow. :gurgling noises:
  • Jack - Nicolas Andre (3.0)
    What should be the mastermind and head-honcho of this league with his combo of Bijan and Saquon turned into a dud. I mean who drafts a Jet... at all! But to do it in the 3rd round is wow. But that wasn't the worst part- he actually dared to draft Lamar Jackson. The player that single handedly screwed my entire season last year, and he drafted him next in the 4th! He's going to be stuck with a lot of fantasy "I.O.U.s" if you know what I mean from that. At least he got Jayden Daniels who for some reason doesn't get the fantasy love he should get. Big risks in Marvin Harrison and BTJ though and then taking a defense at #152 is not exactly knowing that your bread plate is on the left!
  • Leary - Pennsylvania Cop (5.2)
    Killer picks man! Well at least for the first four picks, however, just in the scene after things went a little downhill for Sean in this year's draft. Again, why are we drafting Jets players? Do you want to win or feel like you're drinking piss in the end? I have mixed feelings on this whole lineup but I'm in the same division as him and I'm gonna keep my mouth shut if I know what's good for me buddy. So yeah, let's just keep driving down the road here.
  • Seph - Ski Lift Kids (5.0)
    The kids only line in this movie is "Let's go to the top this time!" and maybe that is what Seph was thinking drafting this team. I didn't like his first picks that much at all as they will help him out about as much as those kids helped out Harry- but then he went on a pretty nasty second half of the draft run. Tate, Lawrence, Godwin, Concepcion, and Likely are all great value picks. He's not in juggernaut territory by any means (as the kids aren't either in stature), but he might be able to get a cup of warm water and melt his playoff drought with a solid foundation in the coming years.
  • Cerca - Mrs. Neugeboren (4.0)
    Ya might want to run a comb through this lineup as Cerca went for some obvious reaches. I loved Jordan Tyson in college at ASU but he also burned me a little with injuries in those leagues and was happy he didn't fall to me at some point to where I'd have to deal with that again. Price, Lloyd, and Lemon- that's a big lack of exeperience that also goes with Nabers missing large chunks of time too. Like hiring Harry as your personal dog transporter, I wouldn't want to have that much risk involved in something so important.
  • Tommmy - FBI Special Agent Beth Jordan (8.3)
    Tommy lost in the playoffs last year so he decided to come home and wanted to fix the sink, I mean his team. Two dominant keepers (both of 'em?) in Taylor and Brown make him dangerous as Beth is with her gun (and really her looks too if we're being honest). The guy then took some top end volume WRs and then got Mahomes at pick #112?? Hey?! Who!? But... Huh?!? Don't ask me how he pulled that off. The only thing he lacks is a supporting cast, kind of like Beth who just lost her boyfriend too. But in the end, this one is pretty solid.
  • SOB - Hawaiian Tropic Oil Boys (10.0)
    I mean I wasn't going to try for 2026, I serious wasn't... was going to try to load up for a 3-year run, but then you all let me get Josh Allen for free basically. Then Tet and Wan'Dale and I was still able to load up on potential keepers at RB for future years. I felt like one of the luckiest guys- like the two guys that ACTUALLY got to become the oil boys in the end. No matter what I do I just always end up lucking into being the best drafter.
